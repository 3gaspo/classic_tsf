\section{Objective}

Classic TSF will evaluate TimeTensors- and RevIN-like supervised forecasting
experiments on Electricity, Traffic, Solar-Energy, Weather, Exchange Rate, and
the four ETT datasets. PEMS is excluded. Every dataset is stored once in TIME's
multivariate saved-Arrow representation and consumed through the common TIME
loader.

\section{Comparison contract}

All methods will share chronological training, validation, and held-out test
regions, training-window sampling, lookback lengths, forecast horizons,
objectives, and run seeds. These choices remain intentionally unspecified and
will be fixed before model implementation. Ports preserve the corresponding
source experiment behavior unless an explicit scientific change is recorded.

\section{Evidence boundary}

No result is claimed until the shared protocol, model ports, and baselines are
implemented and the project-owned artifacts are completed and inspected.
